# RAHA Design

## Inspiration

I looked at interior design references on Pinterest. I liked the warm neutral colors, natural wood textures, and simple room styling. I adapted those ideas for RAHA by pairing a warm palette and serif headings with room scenes that link to matching products. This lets visitors see how pieces work together and explore them in the catalogue.

## Visual style

- Use soft white and warm ivory for backgrounds, and charcoal brown for text and primary buttons.
- Use greige, taupe, and warm grey for borders, panels, and supporting details.
- Pair serif headings with simple, readable body text.
- Keep spacing, button styles, and image corners consistent across pages.
- Use individual product photos in the catalogue and furnished room scenes on the Room Inspiration page.

## Layout and accessibility

- Keep the homepage focused on the opening image, six featured products, the room inspiration slider, and the registration demo.
- Keep search, category filters, and price sorting together on the Products page.
- Start with the mobile layout and adapt it for wider screens at 768px and 1280px.
- Use semantic HTML, one main heading per page, and a label for every form field.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
