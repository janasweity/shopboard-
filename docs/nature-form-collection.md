# Nature & Form collection

All 12 product photographs and three furnished-room photographs were generated with the built-in image_gen tool and saved to `public/images/collection/`. They are illustrative concept products. No screenshot, interface, branding, or Pinterest elements are used.

## Reference study

- Upper-left room: cream curved seating, olive chairs, low stone table, neutral rug, olive abstract artwork, and greenery.
- Pendant study: rounded asymmetric beige shells and softly lit undersides.
- Mirror vignette: irregular full-length mirror, broad-leaf plant, ribbed neutral planter, and warm illumination.
- Lower-left room: generous modular sectional, open-plan proportions, layered tables, natural wood, and rugs.
- Lower-middle-left material board: rounded upholstery, pleated/sculptural light, warm wood, ceramic accessories, and plant silhouettes.
- Lower-middle-right material board: tactile beige stone and textiles, cream curves, olive accent seating, plants, and light wood details.
- Right-hand collage: fluted dark wood sideboard, organic mirror, sculptural cream seating, olive mushroom lamp, abstract art, curved rug texture, and ring-shaped ceramic vase.
